About
Hello. I am Kenny — a media practitioner with more than 10 years of experience, and a lecturer in community college.

I am the co-founder and Chief Editor of Wave Zine HK, an online publication about Hong Kong popular culture. We conduct in-depth interviews with directors and musicians, while also experimenting with new forms of storytelling — from AI-assisted projects to interactive data visualisations.

I also make small interactive tools for teaching, using technology not simply as a convenience, but as a way to make students think, participate and see familiar ideas differently.
