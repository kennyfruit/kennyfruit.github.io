Hi, I'm Kenny.

I am a media practitioner with more than 10 years of experience, and a lecturer in community college. I am the co-founder and Chief Editor of Wave Zine HK, an online publication about Hong Kong popular culture.

At Wave, we conduct in-depth interviews with directors and musicians, while experimenting with new forms of storytelling, including AI and interactive data visualisation. I also create small interactive tools for teaching, using technology to encourage participation and make ideas easier to explore.
